! ==========================================
! HOW TO ADD RULES
! ==========================================
!
! Block a specific URL:
! ||example.com/path/to/file.js
!
! Block a JavaScript file:
! ||example.com/path/to/file.js$script
!
! Block an entire domain:
! ||ads.example.com^
!
! Block a third-party resource:
! ||example.com/file.js$third-party
!
! Block a resource only on a specific website:
! ||example.com/file.js$domain=website.com
!
! Add exceptions (allow a blocked resource):
! @@||example.com/file.js
!
! RULE GUIDELINES:
! - Add new rules under the relevant website section.
! - Use ! to add comments.
! - Use || to match a domain and its subdomains.
! - Use ^ as a separator/end-of-hostname marker.
! - Use $script to target JavaScript resources.
! - Keep one filtering rule per line.
! - Avoid blocking entire domains when a specific URL is sufficient.
